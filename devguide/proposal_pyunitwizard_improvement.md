**Proposal: Capture pyunitwizard Ideas Here**

`pyunitwizard` is the shared unit engine for this ecosystem. If you ever need more
conversion helpers, better quantity detection, or helper utilities while implementing
TopoMT methods (for instance, binder coordinate normalization or multi-dimensional
units), use this document to note the requirement and suggest it for the upstream library.

Explain the scenario (API boundary, data type, target units) and why a central helper
would be preferable to yet another project-local helper. This allows us to consolidate
unit handling requests and carry them upstream in a coordinated way.
