## Historical statements

For a previously observed limitation, name the affected version and the correcting
source/release boundary. A limitation resolved in the target profile cannot be
presented as a present-day weakness merely because its original failure remains
in a notebook, old comment or report. A source correction does not imply delivery
in a different or older installed artifact.

## Final review

Confirm current summaries, fixing commits, issue dispositions, applicable tests,
independent references and comparability. Record uncertainty and remaining scope.
Repeat this review after relevant changes and before submission/revision. A green
metadata checker alone is not approval of the proposed scientific wording.
