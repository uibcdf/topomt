# Descriptive finding title

**Finding ID:** `stable-descriptive-id`
**Kind:** `dfnd_advantage` / `third_party_limitation` / `dfnd_limitation`
**Lifecycle:** `candidate`
**Owner:** TopoMT maintainers or delegated owner
**Reviewed on:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Current summary and software scope

State the latest conclusion and its exact input/model, DFND/provider revision,
probe/query and evidence scope. For a resolved limitation lead with the correction
and fixing revision; distinguish source delivery from installed release support.
For needs_review say what is unknown and what will settle it. Do not leave a
historical failure phrased as a current general limitation.

## Question, independent reference and observed result

Describe the problem and intended user. Link the frozen input, preparation,
atomic radii/boundary conditions, protocol, reference, original observations and
units. Distinguish source-reported evidence, our reproduced behavior and our
interpretation. Link source/software/resource receipts and method conversations.

## Comparative meaning and limitations

What exactly does this demonstrate? Are the inputs/operators eligible and
compatible? Identify metric and comparison scope. A crash, valid empty result,
unsupported input, definition difference and geometric error are distinct.
Avoid unsupported claims of novelty, superiority or biological relevance.

## Owning action and resolution

Link the issue/report for an actionable DFND limitation and for every needs_review
entry, including third-party investigations. Pending work must remain visible
through an open owning/follow-up issue. When resolved, retain
fixing revision, exact resolution scope, verification evidence and executable
regression guard. Partial fixes do not resolve a broader finding. For a retained
limitation record its accepted reason/decision and observable revisit condition.

## Dated history

- YYYY-MM-DD — Initial observation/state, exact scope and evidence.
- YYYY-MM-DD — Later state change, reason, correcting evidence and delivery scope.

Preserve earlier observations as history. Keep this card's current summary,
hub view, register, case/method summaries and owning issue/report coherent.
Before manuscript reuse follow the target-specific review in
[the operating instructions](https://github.com/uibcdf/topomt/blob/main/devguide/comparative_findings.md).
