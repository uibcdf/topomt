# Dumbbell BenchLab establishment

## What

The maintainer selects `dumbbell` before the later joint six-case review #187.
This supersedes the earlier tube-first scheduling gate while preserving all
open #180/#181/#184/#76 profiles and prior evidence. The selected catalog input
has 94 full-precision argon balls, radii 1.88 Å, nominal lobe radius 7 Å with centers
12.5 Å apart, sampling 3.5 Å, jitter 0.1 Å and seed zero.

## How

Freeze input, units, source IDs/PDB topology and probes 1.4/2.2/6 Å before native
receiving. Independently check complete all-atom paths, a closed polygonal
barrier and its neck separator, and a sufficient finite-hull cover when possible.
Retain unavailable proofs and bound their conclusions to the named anchors and
sets. A reference barrier is not automatically the native convex hull.
Inspect actual raw/typed/public results, source support, hierarchy candidates,
anchor correspondence, native contacts and detached real Ackredit attribution.
Deliver independent system/DFND notebooks and a portable reader/download.

## Why

The case separates communication, enclosure, probe-induced fragmentation and
capacity hierarchy. A two-chamber descriptor inside one native component differs
from two physical components after a neck closes. More components at a larger
probe can be physical fragmentation; component count need not be monotone.

## What was refuted

Construction names, ideal smooth-sphere geometry, catalog labels and old native
regressions are not independent physical references. Two enclosed distinct
anchors prove at least two regions, not exactly all components. A failed path
search proves neither enclosure nor separation.

## Scope and exclusions

One existing symmetric input and case-guided source characterization. No native
scientific retuning, other new geometry, original provider/server, protein work,
exact free-volume count/measure or installed/platform claim. #187 owns the later
joint review; #16/#60/#76 and existing findings retain their distinct scopes.
