# Wide straight-tube study

## What

The maintainer selects `straight_tube_wide_sparse` as the second straight-tube
BenchLab unit. Preserve the narrow/dense unit's evidence and unresolved #180/#181
while characterizing this existing input with independent system/native stages.

## How

Freeze the 77-ball full-precision model, original PDB topology, source IDs,
1.88 Å radii and all query/reference/resource policies before native receiving.
Use probes 1.4 and 2.5 Å shared with the companion, plus a 5 Å exclusion control.
Independently verify whole-axis segments and outward tails, a sufficient hull
cover and bounded complete lateral-wall/end-face/seam certificates. Retain
failed/unresolved attempts. Receive actual raw/typed/public results, supports,
unvalidated candidate paths and detached real Ackredit attribution.

## Why

The wider lumen offers a complementary many-cell positive passage control.
The nominal ring radius is 6 Å and nominal sampling 3.5 Å, versus 3.5/2.5 Å
for the narrow input; both radius and sampling change, so their comparison is
not a controlled experiment isolating one parameter. Catalog names and old
channel/pocket labels are not independent physical truth.

## What was refuted

A sparse atomic array does not automatically have physical side-wall openings.
A successful axis is one witness, not the optimal path; a failed witness is not
proof of closure. An empty finite carrier does not exclude unbounded exterior
space. Native links, Mouth children and strict-free hull patches differ.

## Scope and exclusions

One existing synthetic input, independent references and public/source receiving.
No native scientific operator retuning, original-provider/server submission,
protein characterization, general superiority, optimal path/aperture area or
installed/platform qualification. Prior narrow findings remain under #180/#181,
wider references #76 and source/support qualification #16.

## Acceptance criteria

Commit inputs/protocols before reference/native captures and reader protocols
before fresh kernels. Reuse shared scientific receiving and independent checkers;
guard source/radius/unit/map/complete-support behavior without substituting a
catalog label for an oracle. Deliver separate readable executed notebooks,
figures, case navigation, source-bound ZIP and matching outside-kernel receiving.
Run bounded appropriate tests, Ruff/scoped typing, report/finding and strict
docs/navigation checks; reconcile issues/cards with any observed discrepancies.
Publish [skip ci] commits and verify actual hosted pages and download bytes.
