# Joint six-case BenchLab review

## What

The maintainer requests dumbbell establishment first (#186), then a joint review
of regular tetrahedron, closed shell, shell with opening, narrow/dense tube,
wide/sparse tube and dumbbell. This is the owner of that deferred review, not
an assertion that previous scientific targets pass.

## How

Review frozen inputs, radii/units, independent reference scope, actual raw/typed/
public receiving, physical boundaries/attachments, local versus all-atom
candidates, communication/separation and chamber/throat hierarchy. Inspect
recoverable supports/relations, thresholds, measurement availability, attribution
and notebook/hosted/download presentation. Reuse #180/#181/#184/#76/#60/#16
for their existing themes; split new independently closable findings into issues.

## Why

A collection-wide disposition should distinguish established behavior from
remaining errors, unavailable results, representation choices and references
that prove only named anchors or finite supports.

## What was refuted

Publishing a new case does not resolve older limitations. A greater component
count at a larger probe can reflect fragmentation rather than a violation of
free-set inclusion. Source fixes do not repair earlier frozen results or qualify
an installed matrix.

## Scope and exclusions

The six-case joint review follows #186. No original-provider/server execution,
new geometry/protein characterization, silent retuning/input changes, deletion
of original evidence or general superiority claim.

## Acceptance criteria

Produce a traceable cross-case disposition with exact reference/source profiles,
established results, remaining failure/unknown owners, proposed correction order
and meaningful acceptance guards. Reconcile corrected findings across their
cards/hub/lifecycle/cases/reports and preserve historical evidence. Current
method documentation and study presentation must reflect the resulting scope.
