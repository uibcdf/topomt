## What

The three studies are a strong, reproducible starting panel. They are complete
for their explicitly bounded initial controls, **not exhausted as development
cases or a complete qualification of DFND**. No new source/reference mismatch
was reproduced by the selected panel. Known physical and numerical limitations
remain visible and actionable.

The structure is appropriate: system/reference, native observations and eligible
original comparisons have different jobs. Full-precision ball inputs, declared
units, fixed tolerances, spatially selected origin regions, preserved atom
correspondence and independently verified witnesses make this substantially
stronger than checking detector labels or a plausible image. An actual public
Topography receiving stage already exists outside the native notebooks.

The principal reader problem is temporal: later results are explained correctly
on companion pages, while the main notebooks retain earlier open questions and
next steps. A reader following only Run All can miss the current conclusion.
The notebooks still execute; reproducibility does not establish currency of
their editorial narrative.

### Recommended reader route under #175

Use the same compact sequence for each case: question and geometry → declared
expectations → native observations → public Topography inspection → later
certificates/current conclusion → eligible comparisons. Put a current status
table and a short dated reading map before historical outputs. Readers should
see the result and its interpretation before large code/provenance sections.

Preserve frozen notebooks and evidence. Companion material or explicitly
versioned successor notebooks can supply updated navigation/public execution;
they must not silently turn an earlier observation into a fresh result. A new
reader stage should distinguish loading reviewed data from recomputing DFND,
run independently, and provide notebook-compatible links. Avoid merging all
stages into one large notebook.

work or declaring current limitations fixed.
