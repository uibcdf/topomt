## Why

The wider lumen offers a complementary many-cell positive passage control.
The nominal ring radius is 6 Å and nominal sampling 3.5 Å, versus 3.5/2.5 Å
for the narrow input; both radius and sampling change, so their comparison is
not a controlled experiment isolating one parameter. Catalog names and old
channel/pocket labels are not independent physical truth.

## What was refuted

A sparse atomic array does not automatically have physical side-wall openings.
A successful axis is one witness, not the optimal path; a failed witness is not
proof of closure. An empty finite carrier does not exclude unbounded exterior
space. Native links, Mouth children and strict-free hull patches differ.

No native scientific operator retuning, original-provider/server submission,
protein characterization, general superiority, optimal path/aperture area or
installed/platform qualification. Prior narrow findings remain under #180/#181,
wider references #76 and source/support qualification #16.
