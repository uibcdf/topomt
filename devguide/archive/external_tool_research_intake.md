# Historical external-tool research intake

## What

Issue #8 collected cavity/topography tools, comparison references and related
applications in comments. The maintained list now lives outside the issue queue.

## How

The catalogue migration under #67 retains all 21 distinct historical URLs in
devguide/external_tools_catalog.md or its linked provider overviews. Duplicate
fpocketR/Pocket-to-Concavity comments remain in the original issue history.
Discussion #68 hosts continuing discovery and comparison; #69–#73 provide
integrated-provider conversations. Reading List discussions #10–#13 remain.

## Why

A broad evolving catalogue has no final scientific completion gate. Permanent
versioned navigation and discussion separate research from independently closable
integration, defect and validation work.

## What was refuted

Closing historical intake does not finish research, endorse every listed tool,
certify provider fidelity or close outstanding defects.

## Scope and exclusions

Supersession of the intake location only. Original comments are preserved.
No algorithm, optional dependency, scientific evidence or implementation schedule
is changed by this closure.

## Acceptance criteria

The maintained catalogue preserves every distinct reference and points to the
permanent research discussion. Issue #8's closure links the new destination and
this archived record. Future actionable work follows the reporting protocol.
