# Original HOLE integration

## What

The maintainer explicitly selects HOLE, POVME and PISA as a three-provider
follow-up. This report owns HOLE independently of assessment #161
and native joint BenchLab review #187. Original CLI pore profiles with explicit steering, radii and stochastic seed.

## How

Inspect exact original source/dirty state, licence and dependencies. Preserve
original scientific code. Add test-first options, result decoding, original
execution and source-bound persistence. Keep physical quantities explicit,
inputs/resources/producer identities recoverable, and no-cost eligibility honest.

## Why

Expose a complementary original observable through a composable public API,
without relabelling it as a native DFND feature or claiming biological function.

## What was refuted

A source checkout or protocol fixture does not establish genuine execution.
A public repository does not by itself establish complete licence permissions.
An external runtime does not change TopoMT's supported interpreter contract.

## Scope and exclusions

Bounded original route and engine-free recovery, optional attribution and
focused documentation/evidence. No new native DFND protein work or BenchLab
comparison. Broader fidelity #19 and installed/public qualification #101/#16
retain separate ownership. Preserve all earlier frozen evidence and human edits.

## Acceptance criteria

Require original/direct agreement on small frozen controls, valid empty/error
semantics, wrong-source/resource/query guards, quantities under non-default unit
policies, and real Ackredit completion/branch/reuse/absence/failure/saved reading.
Update catalogue, canonical resource conversation and assessment ownership.
Measure serial execution, run scoped quality checks and archive on completion.

## Resolution — 2026-11-11

The bounded external HOLE adapter, source-bound original result model,
engine-free recovery, optional attribution and user/developer guides are delivered
in source `a816e7a712d1f1da1653114a185b375f846018a2`. Read the
[exact source/build/execution checkpoint](../specialist_provider_checkpoint.md)
and [validation receipt](../evidence/specialist_provider_validation_v1.json).
