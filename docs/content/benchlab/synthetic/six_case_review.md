## Read the observations at the right scale

There are three related levels:

1. **Independent geometry:** an analytic clearance, complete cover, checked path
   or separator establishes a specified physical statement on a declared support.
2. **Native DFND:** local capacities construct wet/dry banks, exterior links and
   provisional motifs. These records preserve recoverable source supports.
3. **Public Topography:** selected native components become classified features
   and native exterior links can become child `Mouth` objects.

A native component, a public feature and a physical connected region need not
have matching counts. In particular, a public `Mouth` counts a compatibility
link, while the separate physical view counts free patches on the finite hull.

## A concrete explanation of the tube candidates

The independent covers already prove that no strict-free probe center exists
anywhere in the narrow 2.5 Å or wide 5 Å finite hull. We now also inspect the
stored residence centers, their four local atoms and every captured atom.

| Profile / native parent | Resident centers checked | Current public parent / child | Point diagnosis |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Narrow 2.5 Å, WET-1 | 24 | OCV-1 / MOU-1 | Every center is blocked by a nonlocal atom. |
| Narrow 2.5 Å, WET-2 | 6 | GRV-1 / MOU-2 | Every center is blocked by a nonlocal atom. |
| Narrow 2.5 Å, WET-3 | 0 | None | Connector-only support; this point diagnostic makes no assertion about it. |
| Wide 5 Å, WET-1 | 1 | OCV-1 / MOU-1 | The center is blocked by a nonlocal atom. |

For example, narrow tetrahedron 64 has source vertices **[4, 67, 76, 77]**.
At its stored center, the four-atom clearance is **7.016483499 Å**. Including
source atom **31** changes the all-atom clearance to **−0.358693175 Å**: that
center lies inside a bare atomic ball, before adding the 2.5 Å probe.
The local value recomputes correctly, and the center lies on the tetrahedron's
edge within the declared support tolerance.

Wide tetrahedron 115 has source vertices **[2, 45, 56, 57]**. Its local clearance
is **5.299843470 Å**, but atom **24** limits its stored center to
**−1.575928567 Å**. Both examples are recoverable from the captured coordinates,
radii and source maps.

This explains the observed local versus all-atom discrepancy without diagnosing
a numerical error in those stored local values. It is not a completeness proof
for the residence solver on arbitrary tetrahedra.

### Why checking the center is only a diagnostic

A failed stored center does not exclude a different free point in its tetrahedron.
Conversely, one clear center does not establish a connecting path or a whole
component's topology. The independent complete covers, rather than this point
test, establish the two tube exclusion controls.

The same diagnostic finds both clear and blocked local centers in positive
shell/tube/dumbbell profiles. This does not refute their independently checked
origin or chamber statements. It shows why removing every cell with a blocked
stored center would need its own scientific justification. Counts of sampled
centers are not volume fractions or an accuracy score.

The {download}`complete diagnostic summary <artifacts/six_case_review_v1/summary.json>`
retains each profile. The API currently preserves local graph findings;
**an all-atom public promotion/qualification policy is still open under
[#181](https://github.com/uibcdf/topomt/issues/181)**. This review changes neither
that policy nor the independent exclusion references.

## Supports, measurements and hierarchy

All 19 fresh runs pass the existing public study checks for complete wet/dry
node partition, source-indexed tetrahedra, resident/connector partition,
reciprocal coasts, native depth regions and public parent/support correspondence.
The archive retains the complete observations and original method attribution.

These checks qualify that native/public route on these inputs. They do not
turn carrier tetrahedra into exact free-volume boundaries, dry banks into
supplied molecular participants, or candidate lining atoms into actual contacts.
Catalog confidence describes the classification rule and is not a probability
that a candidate is physically valid.

The original conditional volume references apply to their frozen domains and
observables. This review does not recompute their integrals or qualify exact
volumes, aperture areas or globally optimal bottlenecks for the tubes/dumbbell.
The dumbbell's chamber/throat dictionaries reach the parent `.motifs`; they do
not become standalone Chamber children or certified physical subvolumes.
