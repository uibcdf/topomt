## Independent proof objects

The reference barrier P is a simple closed chain of polygonal frusta, with
positive intermediate radii and matched seams. It has 994 vertices and 1,984
triangular faces; each edge is shared by exactly two faces. Both anchors and
their axial connecting witness lie inside P. Every entire boundary triangle is
covered by actual expanded atomic balls at 1.4 Å. Complete binary trees retain
both children and the selected split edge; leaf-vertex inequalities extend to
whole simplices by ball convexity. Reference vertices are not extra atoms.
The wall's minimum covering margin is 0.024980397917 Å.

All complete path segments are checked against every atom. The minimum
clearance is 1.432602185630 Å. Along with P's cover this proves a common enclosed
region containing both anchors. At 2.2 Å the complete polygonal neck section at
x=0 is covered by 32 root triangles (minimum leaf covering margin
0.218079290100 Å). The outer wall remains covered, so the two free anchors
belong to distinct enclosed regions. This is an at-least-two statement, not
an exact global physical component count.

The native finite carrier H is the atomic-center convex hull, not P. At 6 Å,
128 face-fan tetrahedra partition H and have a complete all-atom covering
certificate (minimum leaf margin 0.028892206881 Å). Thus H contains no strict-free
center and its physical free-boundary patch count is zero. Unbounded exterior
space persists. These are sufficient constructive witnesses/covers, not exact
volume, reconstructed physical surfaces or optimal bottleneck calculations.
