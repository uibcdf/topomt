## Decision for the next system

**The selected margin-separated questions on these three inputs have sufficient
evidence to admit the next independently specified synthetic study.** No new
geometry or protein characterization is started by this checkpoint. A next
system must freeze its own input, independent predictions, observables and
budgets before native observation; inherited guards remain required.

The explicit unavailable quantities above do not block that next development
question because none is needed to assert the current access/enclosure,
physical-patch or conditional-volume conclusions. If a later case or claim
requires exact aperture area, global bottleneck optimality, general continuous
navigation, public persistence or participant interfaces, its relevant open gate
becomes a prerequisite. Do not treat this disposition as completion of #76,
#60/#61, every synthetic system or the molecular phase.

No demonstrated superiority over other methods is inferred. Pending diagnostic
and eligibility issues stay on the issue board, and the maintained
[comparative-findings register](https://github.com/uibcdf/topomt/discussions/154)
remains the authority for any future comparative claim.
